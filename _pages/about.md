I am a **Software Engineer** at Golden Harvest Infotech Limited and an **AI Researcher** based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My work bridges the gap between scalable enterprise software engineering and adaptive machine learning systems, with a focus on healthcare domain applications, secure computing, and hardware integrations.

I graduated with a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering from Sylhet Engineering College, which is affiliated with Shahjalal University of Science & Technology (SUST). 

Research Interests
======
* **Federated Learning & Healthcare Data Security:** Engineering privacy-preserving machine learning frameworks to handle sensitive, distributed clinical records safely.
* **Agentic AI & RAG architectures:** Building autonomous LLM-driven pipelines and robust Retrieval-Augmented Generation workflows.
* **Computer Vision:** Designing deep learning configurations for regional pattern analysis and custom hardware integration layers.

Professional & Academic Highlights
======
* **Industry Systems:** Led backend engineering pipelines for distributed microservices patterns, including an AI-integrated Hospital Management System and a high-throughput industrial DSLR imaging pipeline handling 1,000+ legal records hourly via native hardware SDKs.
* **Publications:** Co-authored research featured in **Springer** (BLDAR ensemble learning approach for energy consumption optimization) and **IEEE** (Convolutional Neural Networks configuration for tribal textile classification).
* **Leadership & Community:** Served as General Secretary of the Computer Society, Vice President & Instructor for the university photography club (SECPA), and Campus Director for Bohubrihi.
